# Article-specific image diversity

Owner decision: 2026-09-20. Multiple articles looked visually similar; each new article should feel visually new. This supersedes treating newly generated bytes alone as sufficient novelty.

## Production procedure

Before selecting or generating the cover and three inline images, inspect scene briefs, provenance and available local media for the latest five articles, including reservations. Five is an initial bounded lookback, not a claim that older duplication is acceptable. Record actual article identities and any missing history; never claim a comparison that was not performed.

For each of the four images record its adjacent reader question, explanatory purpose, visible subject/action, setting, composition, insertion point, source/generation provenance and final asset hash. Compare this plan against recent scenes. Generic people checking a phone, papers on a desk, calendars and preparation tables are not default filler for unrelated topics. Change the explanatory scene, not just lighting or color. Images within one article must also contribute different information.

Do not reuse earlier article assets. Cropping, recoloring or regenerating substantially the same subject/action/composition does not make a fresh image. Hash comparison can reveal identical files but cannot certify visual or semantic novelty. Production review of local assets and briefs makes that judgment; no automated similarity detector is claimed implemented. Replace inadequate candidates before uploading, within existing tool/cost authorization.

Preserve official-first sourcing, clear reuse rights and visible attribution. Look for another relevant permitted official view if one was already used; if no suitable nonduplicative official option is available, use the established environment-visible photorealistic fallback. Do not invent documentary or official evidence merely to satisfy diversity.

## Boundaries and acceptance

- Four article-specific assets with distinct explanatory roles and an honest recent-history comparison record.
- No known reused file or cosmetic/near-identical scene substitution.
- Existing fact, rights, provenance, alt, cover, ordering and delivery checks remain.
- Local production selection does not restore excluded remote image-pixel/lightbox, print, narration, Lighthouse or external-ad audits.
- No retrospective replacement of public or reserved media, including92, without scoped owner direction.

Rejected alternatives: merely requesting a new generation each time (fresh bytes can still look repetitive); replacing official images with invented documentary scenes (misleading); adding a paid similarity service (not justified or authorized).
